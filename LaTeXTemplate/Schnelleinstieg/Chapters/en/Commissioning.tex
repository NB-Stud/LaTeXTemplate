\section{Commissioning}

Place the lid onto the flower pot, position the decorative stones, and fill the pot with water up to the indicated level. Then connect the permanently attached power cable to a wall socket.

Download the \textit{nRF Connect} app from the App Store, enable Bluetooth on your smartphone, and switch on the power supply box using the toggle switch.  
If the green LED lights up and the blue LED starts blinking, the device is ready for pairing. Once the connection is successfully established (blue LED remains steadily lit), the app displays the current water level.
