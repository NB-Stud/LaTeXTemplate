\section{Setup}
The system consists of a flower pot with a lid, a built-in acrylic tube, and a power supply box containing the control electronics. The pump transports water upward, creating the floating faucet illusion. The ultrasonic sensor is housed in a protective tube and measures the water level inside the pot. The power supply box includes an Arduino Nano 33 BLE Sense for control, three MOSFETs (for the pump, green LED, and blue LED), as well as an integrated power supply unit to provide the necessary voltages.
