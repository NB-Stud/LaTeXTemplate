\section{Safety Instructions}
\begin{enumerate}
	\item For indoor use only.
	\item Refill water regularly; do not operate the pump when dry.
	\item Do not exceed the maximum fill level.
	\item Check the Bluetooth connection before use.
	\item Do not open the power supply box!
	\item Use only the original power supply unit.
	\item Keep all electronic components dry; do not modify the device.
	\item Keep children away from the device when unsupervised.
	\item In case of malfunction (smoke, noise, or odor): switch off immediately and unplug the device.
\end{enumerate}
